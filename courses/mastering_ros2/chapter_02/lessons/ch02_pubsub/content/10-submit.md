## Submit your project

The final submission runs every checkpoint again, plus **hidden tests** on inputs you have not seen:
extreme values for the subscriber and a longer endurance run for the publisher.

Press **Submit project**. Your files are snapshotted and graded in isolation, and the result is saved
to your progress and portfolio. Submit as often as you like. Each submission is kept.

### What you practised
* writing timer-driven publishers and callback-driven subscribers in C++ (rclcpp)
* building ament_cmake packages with colcon, Ninja and MSVC on Windows
* reading the live ROS graph, message rates and QoS profiles
* reproducing, diagnosing and repairing a QoS incompatibility

The original book's chapter continues with custom messages, services, actions and launch files.
Those are the next lessons in this chapter.
