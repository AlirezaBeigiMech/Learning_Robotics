## Check forward kinematics

**Check: Live TF** launches your package, publishes random joint angles, and asks TF where
`grasp_frame` is in `base_link`. That is **forward kinematics**, computed by robot_state_publisher from
*your* model. The grader compares the answer with the specification's kinematics to within 2 mm.

You can do the same lookup yourself in the **Terminal**:

```
ros2 run tf2_ros tf2_echo base_link grasp_frame
```
